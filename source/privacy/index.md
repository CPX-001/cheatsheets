title: Privacy
date: 2026-09-12 00:00:00
---

Sheet Codes contains no advertising scripts, analytics trackers, accounts or third-party comment system. Search runs in your browser. Your theme preference is saved in local storage.

Some technical sheets load functional libraries from public CDNs for formulas, tooltips or code playgrounds. Following an external link or running an example opens the corresponding provider. Those providers and the hosting platform can receive ordinary request information, such as your IP address.
